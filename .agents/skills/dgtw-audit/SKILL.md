# Independent Critic

Read the manifest, normalized input, grouping, stock-level contributions, quarterly summary and `audit.json`. Recompute a sample independently, check exclusion reasons and temporal alignment, and distinguish source assertions from verified facts. Hard failures cannot be waived by narrative. Allow at most two revisions; source changes require a fresh run. Record accepted/rejected status and evidence in `docs/build-log.md`. Human approval is required before external publication or any consequential action.
